## SDI Domain Gear
- [[FOR-A Hanabi Video Switcher]]
	- Video switcher hardware
- [[UltraStudio HD Mini]]
	- Video output device from the ProPresenter iMac via Thunderbolt, create a Fill SDI feed and a Key SDI feed (alpha-channel only) to send to the video mixing board for the livestream.
- [[BlackMagic MiniConverter Audio to SDI]]
	- Adds audio to an existing SDI video signal.
	- Also shows up in Audio Domain Gear
- [[BlackMagic Web Presenter HD]]
	- Encodes the AV stream for livestreaming to YouTube.
- [[BlackMagic Studio HD Mini]]
	- Recording device for the SDI video and audio stream.
- [[BlackMagic MicroConverter SDI to HDMI]]
	- Converts the SDI passthrough (which has passed through [[BlackMagic Web Presenter HD]] and [[BlackMagic Studio HD Mini]]) back to HDMI to be sent back to the Crestron.
- [[Decimator MD-HX]] x2
	- HDMI to SDI converter
	- Seem to be broken.
	- Also appears in HDMI Domain Gear.
