- Main Mixing Console for the church. A full-featured digital mixing board.

# I/O
- Ethernet:
	- [[Allen and Heath AR84]] (dSnake, short ethernet, still in Front of House)
	- [[Allen and Heath GX-4816]] (gigaACE protocol) - cable run along the house right top corner of the room
	- [[TP-Link Archer AX55]]
- Local XLR input:
	- 1–2: PC L/R (computer audio)
- XLR out:
	- 1–12: [[Shure PSM900 Transmitter P9T]]. Carrying IEM mixes 1–6, stereo pairs.
	- 13-14: [[Shure PSM900 Transmitter P9T]]. Carrying IEM mixes 7–8, mono.
- 1/4" out:
	- A: Zoom H4 Recorder (The Well)
	- B: Unused
- RCA input:
	- 45–46: CD player in FoH. We've never used this that I've seen, and it doesn't turn on. Missing a power cable?
	- 47–48: iPod. Connected to an old iPod nano
- RCA output:
	- 25–26: Unassigned (formerly assigned to a stereo audio group, when we don't have any configured)
- SPDIF Output:
	- 27–28: Unassigned
- Digital XLR out:
	- 29–30: Unassigned
