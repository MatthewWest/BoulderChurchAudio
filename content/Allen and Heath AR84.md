An expansion board which accepts 8 XLR inputs with remotely controlled gain and sends 4 XLR outputs.

Located at [[Front of House Components]]
# I/O Connections
- XLR in:
	- [[Sennheiser EW 300 G3]] (x7, Handheld A–D & Headset 1–3)
	- [[Sennheiser EW 100 G3]] (x1, Headset 4)
- Ethernet to the [[Allen and Heath SQ-6]] (dsnake)
- XLR out:
	1. [[QSC Audio PLX1602]] (amp for mothers room)
	2. [[Williams Sound PPA T45]] (hearing assistance radio base station)
	3. [[Mackie 402VLZ4]] (Boulder Church Livestream)
	4. The Well livestream
