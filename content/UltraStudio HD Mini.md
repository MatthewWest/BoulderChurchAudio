This is used to process the video signal from the ProPresenter iMac via Thunderbolt, and create a Fill (lyrics) and a Key (alpha-channel only) to send to the video mixing board for the livestream.

Inputs:
- SDI, *Ref In*: frequency lock signal from the [[FOR-A Hanabi Video Switcher]]
- Thunderbolt 3 cable from [[ProPresenter iMac]]
Outputs:
- *SDI Out A*: Fill (lyrics feed) [[FOR-A Hanabi Video Switcher]]
- *SDI Out B*: Key (alpha-channel only)
