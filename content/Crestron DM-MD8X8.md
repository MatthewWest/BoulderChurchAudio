This is a matrix which can connect HDMI inputs and outputs and HDBT with custom routing.

- Inputs
	- Card 1
		- HDBT In: HDMI In on-stage box
	- Card 2
		- [[Matrox TripleHead2Go]] output #2
	- Card 3
		- HDMI In: [[cmple HDMI 1x2 Splitter]] output #2
	- Card 4
		- HDMI In: [[BlackMagic MicroConverter SDI to HDMI]]
		- HDMI Out: [[Wyrestorm Express 3x1 HDMI Switcher]]
	- Card 5
		- HDMI In: [[CableMatters 4K UHD DisplayLink HDMI Adapter]]
	- HDBT Card
		1. Projectors (not sure which)
		2. Projectors (not sure which)
		3. Projectors (not sure which)
		4. [[Crestron DM-RMC-SCALER-C]] (to [[Sharp TV]])
Projectors listed above are:
- [[Crestron DM-RMC-SCALER-C]]
- [[Panasonic RZ-370 Full HD]] x2
- [[Digital Projection HighLite Laser II]]
